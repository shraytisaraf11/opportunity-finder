About SWAYAM

SWAYAM is an online learning platform supported by the Government of India. It provides courses across different academic and professional subjects.

What can you learn?

  • Programming and Computer Science
  • Engineering subjects
  • Management and Business
  • Mathematics and Science
  • Other academic and skill subjects

Who can learn?

Learners interested in improving their knowledge and skills can explore courses. Some courses may recommend prerequisite knowledge.

Is everything free?

Course learning is free. Optional certification examinations may require a fee. Check the individual course page for current dates and conditions.
