About the Challenge

Seva First Innovation Challenge encourages young innovators to create practical solutions for real-world problems.

Students can participate through eligible educational institutions.

Who can participate?

  • School students in eligible institutions
  • ITI and Polytechnic students
  • Undergraduate and postgraduate students
  • Research scholars in eligible institutions

Important: Students cannot register directly. A nominated institutional coordinator must register and submit entries.

Participating Regions

The current South Zone edition lists Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep and Puducherry.

Check the official website for the latest regional participation information.

Innovation Themes

  • Environment and Sustainability
  • Agriculture and Rural Innovation
  • Health and Inclusion
  • Education and Skills
  • Safety and Infrastructure
