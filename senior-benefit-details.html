About the Scheme

Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana is a Government of India scheme intended to support eligible senior citizens experiencing age-related disabilities or infirmities.

The scheme provides eligible beneficiaries with assistive living devices to support mobility and daily activities.

Who May Be Eligible?

  • Indian senior citizens aged 60 or above
  • People meeting applicable income criteria
  • People with qualifying age-related infirmities
  • Applicants meeting official assessment requirements

Final eligibility must be confirmed through the official scheme guidelines and implementing organisation.

What Support May Be Available?

  • Walking sticks
  • Walkers
  • Wheelchairs
  • Hearing aids
  • Other approved assistive devices

Devices are provided based on eligibility, assessment and applicable scheme guidelines.

How to Access the Scheme?

Check the official ALIMCO website for information about the scheme, beneficiary assessment, distribution camps and available contact channels.

Availability and procedures may vary by location.
