About MyGov

MyGov is a Government of India citizen engagement platform.

It allows citizens to participate in discussions, surveys, contests and other government engagement activities.

How Can You Participate?

  • Join public discussions
  • Participate in surveys
  • Share ideas and suggestions
  • Explore available citizen tasks
  • Participate in relevant campaigns

Why Might Senior Citizens Find It Useful?

Senior citizens can explore opportunities to share their experience, opinions and ideas on public issues.

Activities are not exclusively for senior citizens, and eligibility may vary by activity.

Important Information

MyGov is a citizen participation platform, not a guaranteed volunteering job or paid employment service.

Check the official website for current activities and requirements.
