About NIELIT

NIELIT is an institution under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India.

It provides education and training in electronics, information technology and digital skills.

What Can You Learn?

  • Basic computer knowledge
  • Using the internet
  • Digital literacy skills
  • Computer applications
  • Other technology-related subjects

Actual topics depend on the course selected.

Can Senior Citizens Join?

Interested senior citizens can explore available courses and check the specific eligibility requirements.

Courses are not necessarily designed exclusively for senior citizens.

Important Information

  • Some courses may have fees.
  • Eligibility varies by course.
  • Online/offline availability varies.
  • Registration dates may change.
